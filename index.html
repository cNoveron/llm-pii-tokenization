Acknowledgements and Rationale

Are Vertex AI or Mistral AI Studio GDPR-compliant?

Yes, with important caveats

Both providers satisfy GDPR on paper. Vertex AI backs this with a formal Cloud Data Processing Addendum — data stays in your chosen region, models don't train on your prompts unless you opt in, and Google is positioned as a data processor with the customer as the controller. Mistral, headquartered in Paris, is subject to EU law directly and doesn't carry the jurisdictional baggage of a US-headquartered provider.

The caveat for Vertex AI is structural: Google LLC is the contracting entity, and as a Delaware C-Corp it's subject to the CLOUD Act and FISA 702. The DPA explicitly carves out Law Enforcement Requests — Google will comply with lawful US government demands and notify customers only "where legally permitted." NSL gag orders mean that notification right can be suspended indefinitely. EU Data Residency is also not on by default — it's a paid add-on. For Mistral the risk profile is lower, but their enterprise data processing terms still warrant review before handling client PII in production.

The deeper point: GDPR compliance at the provider level is a necessary condition, not a sufficient one. It governs what the provider does with your data — it says nothing about whether the application is transmitting PII in the first place. That's an application-layer responsibility.

Is it worth showcasing Vertex AI or Mistral in the demo?

TL;DR: It's not that interesting

Swapping Anthropic for either provider is a one-line config change in the LLM client. The tokenization layer, the session store, the PDF extraction, the prompt structure — none of it changes. The LLM client is isolated behind a single interface. The provider is an environment variable.

This means the compliance conversation belongs at the provider selection stage, not at the architecture stage. If Trustate's client base includes EU law firms, the provider choice is a real decision and Mistral's jurisdictional position is a meaningful differentiator over Vertex AI. But that's independent of how the application is built — and this architecture keeps that decision open.
